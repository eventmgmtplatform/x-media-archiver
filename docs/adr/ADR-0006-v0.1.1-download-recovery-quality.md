# ADR-0006 — v0.1.1 bounded download recovery and quality

## Status

Accepted for governed hotfix validation on 2026-10-29.

## Context

A final failed download job legitimately has no artifact. Treating every job as if it required an
artifact poisoned an otherwise consistent repository. Absolute limits below an existing verified
total also failed instead of behaving as idempotent no-ops. Operators needed bounded additive
semantics and an explicit statement of the provider-selected quality policy.

## Decision

- Only `COMPLETED` jobs have a one-to-one artifact requirement. A `FAILED_FINAL` job without
  artifact or filesystem residue is retryable through an explicit domain transition that preserves
  its attempt count. Any artifact/residue mismatch remains fail-closed.
- `download --limit N` is an absolute desired verified total and accepts 1–10. Existing totals at or
  above N return successfully without provider/authentication/network access or deletion.
- `download --add N` accepts 1–10 and computes `existing_verified + N`; a result above ten fails
  before provider construction. `--limit` and `--add` are mutually exclusive; neither means limit 1.
- `--max-scan` bounds observed candidates to 1–500 (default 100), in addition to two bounded workers
  and discovery deadlines.
- `--quality best` is the only v0.1.1 quality value and maps to gallery-dl's best direct Twitter
  video selection. Sidecars and manifests persist `quality_policy=provider-best`, safe observed
  dimensions, MIME, extension, size and hash, never an ephemeral URL.
- One second bounded discovery attempt may refresh a provider-selected URL after a media failure.

`archive` remains the durable checkpointed workflow and remains certified only through total ten.

## Consequences

Failed attempts remain auditable and completed evidence is never removed. Exhaustion is explicit
and bounded. This is not mass-download certification, a scheduler, transcoding, or support above
ten. No SQLite migration or new dependency is required.
